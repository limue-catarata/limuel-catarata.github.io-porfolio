04 / CAPSTONE • CHAPTER I

Technology for
a real-world need.

Development of an AI-Enabled IoT-Based Smart Fish Drying Monitoring System for Reducing Post-Harvest Losses Among Fisherfolk in Barangay Pasil in Support of Sustainable Development Goal 1.

THE PROBLEM

Unpredictable drying conditions

Fisherfolk in Barangay Pasil often rely on open-air sun drying. Rain, cloudy weather, humidity, and changing temperatures can interrupt the process. Manual checking takes time, while spoilage and infestation can affect fish quality, safety, and income.

THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

Monitor, analyze, and notify

IoT sensors will collect temperature and humidity information, alongside weather-related data. AI will analyze the information to help identify potentially suitable or harmful drying conditions and provide timely alerts or recommendations.

CORE FUNCTIONS

Useful information at a glance

  • Real-time environmental monitoring
  • AI-assisted condition analysis and prediction
  • Alerts and recommendations for users
  • Data recording and a monitoring interface
SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Monitoring support, not automation

The study focuses on selected fisherfolk in Barangay Pasil and evaluates the proposed monitoring system using user feedback. It does not directly automate the physical fish-drying process. Sensor, internet, power, and study-time limitations may affect implementation.

GENERAL OBJECTIVE

Develop an AI-enabled IoT smart fish drying monitoring system intended to improve the drying process, reduce post-harvest losses, and support fisherfolk productivity and income.

SDG 1 • NO POVERTY

These are intended benefits of the proposed system, not results already proven by testing.
