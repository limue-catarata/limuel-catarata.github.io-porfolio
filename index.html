01 / The challenge

When the weather
changes, income is at risk.

Chapter I describes how traditional open-air fish drying depends on weather and repeated manual checking. Rain, cloud cover, and humidity can slow drying or affect product quality, creating spoilage, food-safety concerns, and lost income for small-scale fisherfolk.

01

Unpredictable weather

Rain and changing outdoor conditions can interrupt the drying process at critical moments.

02

Constant checking

Fisherfolk must keep watching the fish and the sky, taking time away from other work.

03

Post-harvest losses

Improper drying can cause spoilage before the product reaches the market and reduce earnings.

04

Access and affordability

Advanced monitoring tools can be difficult for small-scale fishing households to access.

02 / System blueprint

A little more foresight.
A lot less guesswork.

The proposed system combines environmental sensing with AI-assisted analysis. It is designed to inform the user—not physically operate the drying process.

Stage 01 · Sense

Collect conditions

IoT sensors gather temperature and humidity readings from the drying area, alongside available weather-condition information.

Stage 02 · Send

Transmit data

Collected readings are sent to the monitoring interface so users can review current conditions and recorded observations.

Stage 03 · Interpret

Analyze risk

The proposed AI component analyzes patterns in the data to predict potentially unfavorable drying conditions.

Stage 04 · Inform

Alert the user

Timely alerts and recommendations help fisherfolk decide when to check, protect, or adjust their drying practice.

04 / Proposed features

Designed around the
people doing the work.

The feature direction follows the needs described in the study: accessible monitoring, practical interpretation, and timely information that supports everyday decisions.

◉

Environmental monitoring

View temperature, humidity, and available weather conditions without relying only on repeated manual checks.

⌁

AI-assisted prediction

Analyze collected conditions to identify patterns and flag possible drying risks for further checking.

↯

Timely alerts

Receive notifications and recommendations when conditions may negatively affect fish drying.

▤

Data history

Keep a readable record of environmental observations to help users review changes over time.

♧

Evaluation-ready

05 / The study

Research with a
real-world purpose.

The capstone focuses on the experiences of fisherfolk in Barangay Pasil and the development and evaluation of a monitoring system grounded in their needs.

Project record

Development of an AI-Enabled IoT-Based Smart Fish Drying Monitoring System for Reducing Post-Harvest Losses Among Fisherfolk in Barangay Pasil in Support of Sustainable Development Goal 1.

The research identifies challenges linked to weather conditions, manual monitoring, post-harvest losses, and food quality and safety. Its intended contribution is to support better-informed drying decisions and reduce avoidable losses.

Cebu Eastern College, Inc. · College of Information Technology · AY 2026–2027

  • 01
